## Customer Interview Script

**Goal:** Understand how electrical contractors currently track compliance and what the real pain is.

**Time:** 30–45 minutes

---

**Opening:**
"Thanks for making time. I'm building a tool for compliance tracking in electrical contracting, but I want to make sure I understand the actual problem before building too much. I'm going to ask some questions about how you currently handle this — there are no right answers, and this isn't a sales call."

**Questions:**

1. How many people do you have working with you right now — employees, subcontractors, both?

2. What kinds of documents do you need to track for your crew? (Let them list — don't prompt with examples)

3. Walk me through how you currently track these. Where does the information live?

4. What happens when something expires? How do you find out?

5. Has an expired license or certificate ever caused a problem for you — like a job stoppage, failed inspection, or lost bid? Tell me about that.

6. How much time do you spend per week on compliance-related admin?

7. If you could change one thing about how you track this stuff, what would it be?

8. Have you ever looked at software for this? What did you find? Why didn't you use it?

9. What would make you pay $50 a month for a tool that handled this for you? What would make you not bother?

10. Is there anything about compliance tracking that you're actually satisfied with?

**Closing:**
"This has been really helpful. I'm building a simple tool that tracks expiration dates and sends reminders. Would it be okay if I shared an early version with you and got your feedback?"

## Objection Handling Guide

**"I already track this in a spreadsheet."**  
Response: "Most contractors do. The main thing spreadsheets don't do is send reminders. You have to check it yourself, which means things slip. Does that happen?"

**"This seems like something my office manager handles."**  
Response: "Does your office manager use a tool for it, or is it more ad hoc? Does she ever miss renewals?"

**"I'm not sure we need this — we don't have that many licenses to track."**  
Response: "Even with 5 people, you could have 15–20 documents with expiration dates once you count licenses, insurance, OSHA training, and vehicle registration. That's a lot to track manually."

**"We've never had a problem with expired licenses."**  
Response: "That's great — hopefully it stays that way. Most contractors say the same thing until the first time it happens. Is it just that you've been lucky, or do you have a reliable system?"

**"What happens to my data if I cancel?"**  
Response: "We keep it for 30 days after cancellation, then delete it. You can export everything as a CSV before canceling."

**"We use [ServiceTitan / Jobber / etc.] for everything."**  
Response: "Does it track document expiration dates and send reminders? Most field service platforms don't — they're built for scheduling and invoicing. CrewCompliant is just for compliance tracking."

**"$49 a month seems expensive for something small."**  
Response: "One work stoppage from an expired license probably costs more than a year of subscriptions. But I get it — only makes sense if you feel the pain. Is compliance tracking actually a problem for you right now?"
